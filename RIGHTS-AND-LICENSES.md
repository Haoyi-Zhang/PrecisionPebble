# Rights and Licenses

The executable artifact is intended for anonymous research review. Before public release or archival deposit, the responsible human authors must select and approve a software/data license that they have authority to grant.

The artifact does not silently grant rights beyond those already stated in files shipped with it. Generated evaluation instances contain no third-party personal or proprietary data. The ACM LaTeX class and bibliography style in `paper/` remain governed by their own upstream notices and are not relicensed by this project.

The release checklist requires the responsible authors to verify: (1) ownership or permission for every source and data file; (2) compatibility of the chosen artifact license with bundled third-party material; (3) venue requirements for anonymous supplementary material; and (4) the final public repository or archival DOI after de-anonymization.
